Support

We read every message.

Email support@joineasy.app and we will get back to you. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps us answer faster.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Easy and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I was charged after my free trial

Free trials convert to a paid subscription unless cancelled before they end. Refunds are handled by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

I paid but the app still says I'm not subscribed

Open Settings › Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you purchased with. If it still does not unlock, email us.

The calories look wrong

Estimates depend on portion size and ingredients the app cannot see. Use Fix it on the result to correct the food or the portion — the totals update, and it helps the app get closer next time.

How do I get my data, or delete it?

Settings › Export Data gives you a copy of what the app holds. Settings › Delete My Data erases your local data and your account. Deletion cannot be undone.

Someone in my group is behaving badly

Tap and hold their message to report it, or block them from their profile. Reports come to us and we act on them. Blocked members disappear from your view of the group.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings › Easy › Notifications, then review Settings › Notifications inside the app, including quiet hours.

Report a problem or request a feature

Email support@joineasy.app. If something is broken, a screenshot and what you tapped just before it happened is enormously useful.
